# HANA TCG — First Player Playtest v0.12.1

## Message to send

Open this on your phone and play one match:

https://mikelninh.github.io/hyperspace-pocket-museum/hana-tcg/

Please don't read anything beforehand or ask me how it works.
After the match, use the tiny feedback form in the game and then tell me anything that confused you.

## What we are testing

Do NOT ask testers to test every mode.

Primary test:
- fresh first-run
- Hana vs Le
- one complete match

Only after that, if they want more:
- another hero
- Duo Draft
- Deck Lab
- Binder

## Success signals

- first meaningful move <45 seconds
- first match completion >=75%
- clarity >=4/5
- play-again intent >=70%
- player remembers at least one card / Scene / character
- no recurring "what can I do now?" confusion
- no recurring mobile visibility problem
- no blocker bugs

## Do not coach

If a tester asks "what do I press?", record the exact moment before helping them.

The product needs to answer.

## Follow-up questions

After they are completely done, useful questions are:

1. What did you think you were trying to do?
2. Where did you feel confused or slow?
3. Which card, character, Scene, or phrase do you remember?
4. Did any turn feel clever or satisfying?
5. Would you play another match without me asking?
6. What did it remind you of?
7. What would make you send it to a friend?

Avoid leading questions like:
- "Did you like the movement system?"
- "Was the art cool?"
- "Was it easy to understand?"

## Evidence hierarchy

PLAYER BEHAVIOR > unsolicited player language > prompted feedback > our interpretation.

A single loud opinion is a lead, not a product decision.

## First cohort

Start with 5 people, inspect results, fix blockers.
Then 10 more.
Then 10 more.

Do not burn 25 testers on the same obvious onboarding bug.
