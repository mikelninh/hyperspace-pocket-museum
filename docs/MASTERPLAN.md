## V1 stop condition

A new visitor can discover all 8 wonders, remember at least one true fact,
keep a personal collection, share a wonder into Telegram, and bring a friend
back into the museum.

## Later supporter system

If paid randomized packs are ever added:

- odds visible before purchase
- exact supply visible
- per-user limits visible
- odds cannot change after a release begins
- meaningful free discovery remains
- no misleading countdowns or dark patterns
- duplicates policy stated in advance
- legal review before launch
